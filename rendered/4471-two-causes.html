#4471 · two causes, not one

#4471 was merged as one issue and marked known. It is two bugs. Both flip a date, but they have different causes and different fixes. One fix does not close the other.

What the table shows

Intake tags each report from the reporter's browser. That tag is a guess. The cause comes from the tenant: the locale it writes dates in, and the booking form it runs. The table below groups every report merged into #4471 by that cause. It counts each report id once.

5 reports read as a one-day move; filed under parser by tenant.

The row marked as not this ticket is the second bug. It has no ticket of its own in the desk. We filed one ticket per bug:

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, so day-first tenants get flipped dates. Issue: what-it-shows#1.
  • hint: booking-v3 prints a month-first hint under the date field. Day-first tenants type what the hint says and the form reads it day first. The fix is the hint or the form, not the parser. Issue: what-it-shows#2.
Intake tags do not tell the causes apart. Some parser reports are tagged hint, and some hint reports are tagged parser. Count by tenant, not by tag.

Why they were merged

The desk merges on subject line alone (decision 0001). Both bugs produce the same subjects, such as "Wrong day on the invite". A subject cannot tell a parser report from a hint report, so the merge hid the second bug behind the first.

Open question

The desk read a few reports as a one-day move, not a month flip. They mention time zones. They stay counted under their tenant's cause above. They may be a third bug, a time-zone one. Read them before closing either ticket.

Lessons

  • Merging on subject counts the same words, not the same bug. Merge on cause.
  • A number in a page comes from a component. This page types none.
